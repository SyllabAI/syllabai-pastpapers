1. **4SD0 has no Paper 2s.** The qualification assesses via Paper 1B / 1C / 1P

   (zero 2-series rows) and by the covers.
2. **4SD0 papers are content-shared with the 2019+ linear sciences.** Spot-check: the
   4SD0/1C 2022-01 per-question mark structure is identical to 4CH1/1C 2022-01 (11

   Bytes differ, so no F10 duplicate conflict arises, but same-session 4CH1/4SD0 QPs are
   the same assessment content under different covers.
